Highlights

Foiled · Precise · Dimensional

Highlights are placed in foil, section by section, for even, considered brightness that starts close to the root. With babylights, the sections get finer and finer until the result reads as light rather than stripes.

The Approach

Good highlights depend on the section. How thick, how close together, how close to the scalp: those decisions set whether a result looks crisp, soft or sun-touched. Before I place the first foil, I look at how your hair falls and how it will look in the coastal daylight you actually live in, not just under salon lights.

Every highlight service includes a toner to refine the final shade, whether that’s a cool, clean beige or a warmer honey. For the finest effect, babylights place exceptionally small sections close to the root, mimicking the light of hair that has spent a childhood in the sun.

Who It Suits

Highlights suit someone who wants brightness that reaches the root, more defined dimension than balayage gives, or who is blending in grey. A partial focuses on the top and around the face, where light hits first. A full places foils throughout for brightness all over.

Questions

How often will I need my highlights refreshed?

Most clients return every eight to twelve weeks, depending on how much contrast there is between the highlights and their natural colour. Finer placement, like babylights, usually grows out more softly.

Partial or full highlights: which do I need?

A partial lightens the top and around the face, the areas people see most, and suits a softer refresh. A full gives brightness throughout, including underneath. We’ll decide at your consultation based on how you wear your hair.

Can highlights help blend grey?

Yes. Fine highlights are one of the gentlest ways to soften the contrast of grey regrowth, so the line is far less noticeable as it grows out. They pair well with a root colour for fuller coverage.

Will my highlights turn brassy?

Every highlight service includes a toner to neutralise unwanted warmth, and cool water and sulphate-free care at home help it last. A gloss between visits keeps the tone true.
