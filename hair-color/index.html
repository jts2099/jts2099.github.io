All-over · Grey coverage · Gloss

All-over colour should never look flat. Each formula is built for your natural level, your grey percentage and your undertone, so the result is rich, glossy and dimensional in every light.

The Approach

Single-process colour, one colour from root to end, is the foundation of a colourist’s craft, and the place where formulation matters most. I formulate with Davines exclusively, considering your natural depth, how much grey there is and where it sits, how porous your ends are, and even the effect of Mendocino’s hard water on how colour takes.

When colour needs more movement, I build dimension into it: a slightly deeper root, a softer end, a few fine foils, or a gloss to add shine and tone. That is dimensional colour, a single shade given depth so it looks like your own hair on its best day.

Who It Suits

All-over colour suits anyone covering grey, deepening or warming their natural shade, or wanting richer, shinier colour without the upkeep of lightening. Root touch-ups keep established colour seamless between fuller visits, and many clients alternate them with a highlight or balayage appointment as part of their colour plan.

Questions

Will this cover my grey?

Yes. Grey coverage is a large part of my work. Resistant grey sometimes needs a dedicated formula or a little extra processing time, and I’ll factor that in. If you’d rather blend grey than cover it completely, highlights or a softer, translucent formula are lovely alternatives.

How often should I touch up my roots?

Most clients covering grey return every four to six weeks. If you’re enhancing your natural colour rather than covering grey, you can often wait longer.

What is dimensional colour?

It’s colour that isn’t a single flat shade. Depth at the root, a softer tone through the lengths, or a few fine highlights give movement and catch the light, so it reads as natural hair rather than a dye job.

Can I go from box dye to salon colour?

Absolutely, though box dye builds up in layers and can behave unpredictably. Please tell me about any home colour at your consultation. Lightening over it may need a colour correction appointment, which requires an in-person assessment first.
