Balayage

Hand-painted · Lived-in · Light-led

Balayage is colour painted by hand, freehand, onto the surface of the hair. It brightens the places where light would naturally fall, leaving no hard lines, and it grows out softly over months.

The Approach

Balayage is about placement more than lightness. I read your cut, your parting, the way you wear your hair and the way light moves across it, then paint each section where the sun would naturally touch it. The result is brightness with depth still underneath, never stripes.

Most of my clients sit between levels 4 and 8. On those brunette and light-brown bases, balayage gives a warm, coastal glow without the upkeep of a full head of foils. Every balayage finishes with a toner so the brightness lands on the tone we chose together, and doesn’t turn brassy.

Who It Suits

Balayage suits someone who wants brightness that looks like their own, wants a longer gap between visits, and is happy with a softer, blended root rather than precise lightness right to the scalp. If you’d like more lift on a darker base, foilyage, which paints inside foils, often gets there with more control. We’ll decide which suits you at your consultation.

Questions

How long does balayage last?

Because there’s no hard line at the root, balayage grows out gently. Many clients refresh every three to four months, often with a gloss or toner between visits to keep the tone fresh. We’ll map out your timing at your consultation.

What’s the difference between balayage and highlights?

Highlights are woven or sliced into foils for a more even, defined lightness from close to the root. Balayage is painted freehand on the surface for a softer, more lived-in result. Many clients end up with a blend of both.

Can I go lighter in one visit?

That depends on your starting point and your hair’s history. Dark or previously coloured hair may need two visits to reach a bright result safely. I’ll always tell you honestly what’s realistic for your hair.

Is a consultation required?

New clients begin with a complimentary consultation, in person or by phone. If you have a history of box dye or previous lightening, I’ll need to see your hair in person before booking.
